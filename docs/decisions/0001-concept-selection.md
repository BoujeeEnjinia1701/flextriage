# 0001: Concept selection

Status: **proposed, awaiting Amish** (FXT-DEC-001 D1). FT1, FT3 and FT2 score within 10 %, so all three go to Amish without a pick.

## Context

The team's iteration 2 (FT1) folds pre-scored flame-retardant polypropylene panels into a three-zone triage bay joined by clip-lock connectors, with magnetic flaps at the transitions. The STEM check shows that the bay weighs 43.1 kg against the deck's 12 kg, that its 1.2 m walls stand a 178 N push only next to the stubs (about 5 to 15 N between them), and that it needs 8.8 x 2.9 m of floor plus approaches (FXT-STM-001 S1, S6, S22).

## Options considered

- FT1 origami panel bay: 176 of 260; 43.1 kg; USD 1,174.23; 312 N at a stub joint; clear printed cues; no privacy.
- FT3 floor marking and belt stanchions: 169; about 267 kg of steel posts (34 N per post); USD 844 to 1,198; almost no fuel; no screen.
- FT2 tensioned fabric screens on a pole frame: 159; 54 to 65 kg; about USD 2,060; privacy to 1.83 m; posts tip at 3 to 8 N.

## Decision

None yet. Recommendation for Amish's weighing: FT1 for the clearest zones and the shared platform, with FT3's floor tape for the path and the 1 m spacing grafted on. FT1 is carried in the drawing set, BOM and build plan so that a constructable design exists at TRL 3; this is not a choice.

## Consequences

If Amish chooses FT3, the pitch moves from a panel bay to marked floor and stanchions (a pitch change), D3 falls away and the BOM is bought parts. If FT2, privacy is met but mass and cost rise and the posts need the same lateral criterion (D7). If FT1, D2, D3, D4, D7 and D10 decide the pack, height, floor statement, lateral criterion and clip holding.
