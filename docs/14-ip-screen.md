## Summary

No live patent found reads on FT1's combination of pre-scored FR twinwall duos, clip-lock joints with pegs, pin-hinged leaves and anchor feet. The connector and folding-partition patents closest to it have expired. The two active patents found are near FT2 (bed-mounted screens) and FT3 (motorized belt barriers) only in field, not in the claimed features read. The search was a desk screen only: no claims charts, no search of design patents, other jurisdictions or pending applications.
