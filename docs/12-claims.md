# FlexTriage Origami Bay claims register

What the bay may say, what it may say only after testing, and what it must never say. Method: STANDARDS section 25.5b. The bay supports triage and IPC practice by giving zones, a one-way path and clear cues; it is a boundary, not a barrier, and controls no infection by itself.

Table 1. Claims by option.

| Option | May claim now (design intent) | Only after testing | Never claim | Limits to state on the product |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FT1 origami panel bay | "Designed to support one-way triage flow and cohort separation as part of the receiving team's IPC practice"; "three zones with numbered, worded signs"; "flame-retardant PP sheet with an NFPA 701 Test Method 1 report" for a lot bought with that report; "wipeable faces" | A measured deployment time; stability to the BIFMA X5.6 push at stub joints; wipe resistance to named disinfectants; reuse cycles | That it prevents infection or transmission, is an isolation room, ward or unit, gives negative pressure, is sterile, kills or eliminates pathogens, is 100 % effective, is medical-grade or approved by any regulator, or protects against any disease; that it is a barrier to droplets or air | Boundary, not a barrier: do not lean, sit or hang anything on a wall; walls tip if pushed between the short panels; indoors or in a tent only; 1 m from heaters, flames and oxygen; off exit routes; magnets: keep pacemakers 10 cm away |
| FT2 fabric screens | As FT1, plus "privacy screens 1.8 m high" | As FT1 | As FT1 | As FT1; posts tip at a few newtons |
| FT3 floor marking and stanchions | "Marks a one-way path and zones" | As FT1 | As FT1; that belts separate cohorts | Belts can be ducked under; posts tip at 34 N |

## On-product wording (proposed, awaiting Amish)

"Triage bay boundary. Supports one-way flow and cohort separation as part of your infection prevention and control practice; it does not stop germs passing. Do not lean, sit or hang anything on the walls. Lay two bags on every anchor foot before hanging the leaves. Indoors or in a tent only, off exit routes, 1 m from heaters, flames and oxygen. Magnets: keep pacemakers 10 cm away. CONCEPT PROTOTYPE: not for patient care until reviewed by IPC and safety staff."

## Release limitation

No prototype is used in patient care until its stability, fire performance, cleanability and the procedure it embeds are reviewed by qualified infection prevention and control (IPC) and safety staff.
