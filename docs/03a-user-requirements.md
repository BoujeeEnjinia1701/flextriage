# FlexTriage Origami Bay user requirements

What the users in FXT-PRB-001 need, written from their side. Priorities are proposals for review with an emergency medical team.

Table 1. User requirements.

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Deployment and assembly | Fast set-up | Two of us, with no training, must stand up a working three-zone bay in under 15 minutes, without tools | High | Team decision gate; EMT lead (P1); volunteer (P6) |
| UR-002 | Performance | Zones and one-way flow | I need intake, assessment and treatment zones in order, one way in and one way out, and 2 to 4 zones when space differs | High | Team deck; WHO AFRO one-way triage; EMT lead (P1) |
| UR-003 | Performance | Boundary between cohorts | Cohorts must be kept apart by a physical boundary and the spacing WHO asks for, while staff can still see across | High | Outbreak coordinator (P3); triage nurse (P2); WHO AFRO |
| UR-004 | Usability | Clear cues for everyone | Any team and any patient must read the zones and the path the same way, across languages and colour vision, without clashing with triage colours | High | Team non-technical risks; patient (P5); START, SALT |
| UR-005 | Safety | Stays standing | The bay must not fall on anyone when bumped in a busy space | High | Triage nurse (P2); Helpful Engineering |
| UR-006 | Maintenance | Wipe clean | Every surface facing a patient must wipe clean with the disinfectants we use | High | Team deck; IPC lead |
| UR-007 | Safety | Fire | It must not add a fire hazard to a tent or hall full of patients | High | Site fire safety officer |
| UR-008 | Logistics | Carry | Two people must carry a whole bay, ideally in two bags under 12 kg in total | High | Team deck; logistician (P4) |
| UR-009 | Environment and durability | Floor space | It must fit the floor we have, about 20 m2 | Medium | Team key assumption |
| UR-010 | Usability | Stretcher and wheelchair passage | A stretcher and a wheelchair must pass every doorway | Medium | Patient (P5); triage nurse (P2) |
| UR-011 | Usability | Privacy | Assessment and treatment should hide the patient from passers-by where possible | Medium | Patient (P5); EMT lead (P1) |
| UR-012 | Environment and durability | Reusable | It must survive 50 or more deployments | High | Team deck |
| UR-013 | Cost | Affordable | A three-zone bay should cost about USD 800, under USD 500 at volume | High | Team deck (value-engineering target) |
| UR-014 | Usability | Easy passage | Passing a transition must be easy, with as little hand contact as possible, and must not trap fingers or feet | Medium | Team deck (magnetic flaps); IPC lead |
| UR-015 | Regulatory | Honest and lawful | Claims must be honest (no medical device claim) and the bay must meet fire, exit and sign rules where it stands | High | Helpful Engineering; regulators |
| UR-016 | Environment and durability | Tent and hall conditions | It must stand on tent and hall floors, hot or cool | Medium | Team technical risks |
| UR-017 | Maintenance | Local repair | A damaged part should be replaceable from sheet and a printer | Low | Logistician (P4) |
